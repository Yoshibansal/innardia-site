Data Deletion Instructions

Our app keeps only the public name and text of comments left on Innardia Studio posts.

To have that data deleted:

  1. Send a direct message to @innardia.studio on Instagram (or to the Innardia.studio Page on Facebook) saying "delete my data".
  2. Include the name you commented under.

We delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply. You can also delete your own comment on the platform at any time.
