Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Innardia Studio makes short educational videos. Our publishing app is used only by Innardia Studio to post our own videos to our own YouTube channel, Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages, and to read and reply to comments on those posts.

What we access

Through the official YouTube, Instagram and Facebook APIs, the app accesses only our own accounts: our own posts, their basic statistics, and the public comments people leave on them (the commenter's public name and the comment text).

How we use it

We use comments only to read and reply to them and to decide which topics to cover next. We use statistics only to improve our videos.

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not contact people outside the platform where they commented.

Storage and retention

Comment text and public names may be kept in a private store for up to 12 months, then deleted.

YouTube API Services

Our app uses YouTube API Services. YouTube's use of data is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the app can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page.

Your choices

To have your data deleted, follow the steps on our data deletion page.
